# Ubuntu recovery startup diagnosis

**Product acceptance: INCONCLUSIVE.** All three sampled recovery processes have zero measured JIT activity after 120-second workload warmup. This supports the next experiment's warmup design; it is not product A/B/A evidence or a performance PASS. Maximum latency still differs materially between the two untraced processes.

[Run 34168127483](https://github.com/thomhurst/Dekaf/actions/runs/34168127483), [raw artifact 10034981068](https://github.com/thomhurst/Dekaf/actions/runs/34168127483/artifacts/10034981068), [predeclared plan](https://github.com/thomhurst/Dekaf/pull/3128#issuecomment-5576387263).

Pins: main `5df2f0d03607389384b5c1466e17812a9084fac9`, product `e85fcb90bf1ba746eadb604f6f1347670eb04b98`, harness `f996fd4cf3ec7d1ce6ef385b6713b822d2ab296c`. Main is unchanged at the end. One GitHub-hosted `ubuntu-latest` VM runs candidate-only `retry:16` in three fresh processes, sequentially: untraced U1, traced T, untraced U2. All other benchmark jobs are skipped.

Runner: Ubuntu 24.04 image `20260831.293.1`, Linux `6.17.0-1022-azure`, four vCPUs on AMD EPYC 7763 hardware. SDK 10.0.400, runtime 10.0.11, Release/net10.0, workstation GC, `DOTNET_TieredCompilation=1`, `DOTNET_TieredPGO=1`, `DOTNET_gcServer=0`. Every process executes 128 complete 50-ms measurements plus a one-second primer, 120 seconds of actual workload warmup, then 20 measured seconds. The tracer is `dotnet-trace` 9.0.652701; the inspector uses TraceEvent 3.2.6 and reports zero lost events.

The boundary is one complete administrative invocation/result observation over 16 groups, including the fixture's coordinator retry and backoff. Load is closed-loop concurrency one. The synthetic connection uses cached wire response objects; network and broker CPU are excluded. CPU/allocation are the child probe process's inclusive counters, including observer bookkeeping; they exclude the separate tracer process. There is no BDN allocation acceptance measurement in this diagnostic.

The final warmup compilation is in second 86 for U1, 61 for T, and 92 for U2. Every last-five-second warmup interval and every measured interval records zero compilations. The 148.023-second trace retains 6,430 parsed events; no JIT events fall between its measured/finalize markers. This differs from the earlier Windows recovery observation and confirms why platform-specific evidence was necessary.

The untraced U2/U1 differences remain: throughput −1.9706%, CPU/call −0.0651%, p50 −3.4169%, p99 +0.6768%, maximum +17.2717%, process allocation −1.1485 B/call. The maximum difference exceeds the full comparison's 5% control-drift tolerance, and the allocation difference exceeds its 1-byte administrative allowance. These candidate-only observations are not used to grant or relax a product gate. Traced p50/maximum also differ from both untraced processes; this experiment does not estimate total tracing overhead because tracer CPU is outside the child-process scope.

Each phase has one measured process and 20 retained one-second intervals. There are no independent product-baseline replicates and no precision claim sufficient for equivalence. Exact per-call tick histograms, maxima, CPU/allocation/GC/JIT/thread-pool and heap/RSS series remain unchanged in the artifact. Twenty measured seconds do not establish long-run leak absence. No interval, process or outlier is discarded or averaged away.
